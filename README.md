# my-personal-site


As I developed the site, I focused heavily on styling and atmosphere:

Visual Identity: I chose a fixed space background (space.gif) combined with vibrant link highlights (#fbff00) and subtle text shadows (text-shadow) to ensure maximum readability over dynamic visuals.

Custom Animations: I implemented CSS @keyframes to create a smooth, continuous spinning decorative element in the top corner of the screen.

Organized Architecture: I designed internal section navigation (#about, #politics, #projects) so visitors could instantly jump between my personal biography, written essays, and hardware repositories like the SCOUT Recon Rover.

🎯 Why I Built the Site
Projects and ideas are only as effective as how well they are communicated. I wanted a platform that reflected who I am: an engineer who breaks complex problems down to their fundamental mechanics, and a writer dedicated to questioning top-down systems and exploring individual liberty.

Instead of leaving my essays hidden in Google Drive folders or my robotics projects isolated in code repositories, this website brings everything into one unified, accessible portfolio for collaborators, peers, and visitors.

🤖 AI Usage
I utilized AI as a collaborative development tool while coding the frontend of this website.

CSS Debugging & Effects: I used AI to troubleshoot fixed positioning, perfect smooth @keyframes rotation syntax, and calculate effective text-shadow values to maintain high text legibility over animated background graphics.

Structure & Refinement: It helped me organize semantic HTML elements cleanly and streamline visual layout choices.

As with all my work, I drove the vision, designed the overall aesthetic, authored all written content and essays, and integrated the codebase myself. AI served as an efficient reference and technical debugging tool.

💡 What I Learnt
CSS Animation & Mechanics: Gained practical experience with keyframes, transforms, z-indexing, and absolute vs. fixed positioning.

UI/UX Contrast Design: Learnt how to balance bold visual choices (like animated backgrounds) with high-legibility typography and text shadows.

Content Integration: Discovered how to effectively curate and present vastly different disciplines — from robotics projects to geopolitical analysis — within a clean, single-page navigation flow.

🌐 Site Content Overview
✍️ Biography: An overview of my background growing up in London, my libertarian philosophy, and my approach to engineering.

🏛️ Political & Geopolitical Essays: Manifestos and analyses covering state subsidies, institutional reform, independent frameworks, and regional histories.

💼 Economic & Religious Perspectives: Research papers exploring UK tax restructuring, domestic markets, and digital discourse.

💻 Engineering Projects: Direct links and previews of hardware and software repositories, including SCOUT-Recon-Rover.
