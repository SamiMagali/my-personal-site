# my-personal-site


As I developed the site, I focused heavily on styling and atmosphere:

Visual Identity: I chose a fixed space background (space.gif) combined with vibrant link highlights (#fbff00) and subtle text shadows (text-shadow) to ensure maximum readability over dynamic visuals.

Custom Animations: I implemented CSS @keyframes to create a smooth, continuous spinning decorative element in the top corner of the screen.

Organised Architecture: I designed internal section navigation (#about, #politics, #projects) so visitors could instantly jump between my personal biography, written essays, and hardware repositories like the SCOUT Recon Rover.

# How this project helps me.
Projects and ideas are only as effective as how well they are communicated. I wanted a platform that reflected who I am: an engineer who breaks complex problems down to their fundamental mechanics, and a writer dedicated to questioning top-down systems and exploring individual liberty.

Instead of leaving my essays hidden in Google Drive folders or my robotics projects isolated in code repositories, this website brings everything into one unified, accessible portfolio for collaborators, peers, and visitors.

# What I Learnt:
CSS Animation & Mechanics: Gained practical experience with keyframes, transforms, z-indexing, and absolute vs. fixed positioning.

UI/UX Contrast Design: Learnt how to balance bold visual choices (like animated backgrounds) with high-legibility typography and text shadows.

Content Integration: Discovered how to effectively curate and present vastly different disciplines, from robotics projects to geopolitical analysis, within a clean, single-page navigation flow.
